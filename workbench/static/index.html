WHAT YOU ARE EXPLORING

A model’s response to structure

A temporal graph is a sequence of networks. Choose a dataset and a prediction model, then change a concept such as bridge width or density. Compare the new prediction with the original.

The figures show what changed in the network, how its history moved, and how the model responded. This measures sensitivity; it does not prove real-world causation.
